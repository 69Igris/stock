                           LETTER TO SECURITYHOLDERS                            
Dear unitholders and shareholders:

We are pleased to announce the proposed simplification
of our corporate structure, pursuant to which holders of equity interests of
Brookfield Infrastructure Partners L.P. (“BIP”)
would exchange their interests for shares in a publicly traded Canadian
corporation, Brookfield Infrastructure Partners Inc. (“BIP Inc.”), and each of
BIP and Brookfield Infrastructure Corporation (“BIPC” and, together with BIP,
“Brookfield Infrastructure”) would become subsidiaries of BIP Inc. (the
“Simplification”).

Holders of limited partnership units of BIP (“BIP Units”, such holders, “BIP
Unitholders”) and class A exchangeable subordinate voting shares of BIPC (“BIPC
Exchangeable Shares”, such holders, “BIPC Shareholders”) will vote on whether to
exchange their BIP Units
and BIPC Exchangeable Shares, respectively, for class A subordinate voting
shares of BIP Inc. (“BIP Inc. Class A Shares”).
If BIP Unitholders approve the Simplification but BIPC Shareholders do not, the
BIP Units will be exchanged for BIP Inc. Class A
Shares and the BIPC Exchangeable Shares will remain outstanding and become
exchangeable into BIP Inc. Class A Shares.

The special meeting of BIP Unitholders will be
held virtually on October 14, 2026 at 9:00am (Toronto time), and the special
meeting of BIPC Shareholders will be held virtually
on October 14, 2026 at 10:00am (Toronto time).
Why you should approve the Simplification
The Simplification generally is expected to be
tax-deferred for Canadian and U.S. investors and completed without any
meaningful cost to the business, while providing securityholders
with the following benefits, among others:
                                                      
  ·  improved consolidated trading liquidity through  
     a single listed security;                        
                                                      
                                                          
  ·  increased demand from current indices and potential  
     additional index inclusion;                          
                                                          
                                                                     
  ·  stronger alignment with long-term capital allocation            
     trends toward indexable and ETF-eligible corporate securities;  
                                                                     
                                                             
  ·  simplified investor analysis, screening, and            
     benchmarking through a single listed reporting entity;  
                                                             
                                                   
  ·  broader access to a larger pool of investors  
     who prefer corporate structures;              
                                                   
                                                                  
  ·  enhanced governance framework and voting rights              
     for Brookfield Infrastructure’s public securityholders; and  
                                                                  
                                                                                
     for BIP Unitholders, preferential dividend tax                             
  ·  rates and elimination of onerous partnership tax reporting forms for many  
     Canadian and U.S. taxable investors.                                       
                                                                                
Taxable Canadian BIP Unitholders will need to file a simple tax election
to obtain tax-deferred treatment, which can be facilitated through
www.bip.taxelection.ca.
