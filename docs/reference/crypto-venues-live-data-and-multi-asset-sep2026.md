# Crypto venues as a live-data and multi-asset rail — Binance, Coinbase, Kraken, Backpack (13-Sep-2026)

Addendum to `snaptrade-multi-broker-plan-sep2026.md` §3. Question from Shaswat (12/13-Sep): US and Indian live data need licences; for crypto, can a SnapTrade-connected exchange provide all the data, or can we plug straight into an exchange's live API "as there is no legal restriction"? Widened to: Binance, Kraken, Coinbase, Backpack and similar tier-1 venues, for crypto **and** fiat/stock assets across data, trading and accounts. Everything below was read on 12–13-Sep-2026 by four parallel research passes; each claim carries its URL; "not found" means exactly that.

## 1. The premise, corrected

Crypto has no statutory market-data regime — no NSE/BSE-style data-vendor policy, no US SIP/UTP fee schedule — so there is no regulator to license from. What remains is contract: every exchange's public feed is free to _call_ (none of the four needs an API key for market data) but each exchange's terms decide what you may _do_ with it, and all four restrict display to third parties or commercial use unless the exchange consents in writing. "Free to call" and "free to show your users" are different rights, and only the first is public.

Two practical points sit on top of the legal one. A crypto price is per venue, not per asset — BTC on Kraken, Coinbase and Binance are three prices, and a portfolio app wants a composite reference price, which is exactly the "generic or fair value price" Coinbase's terms forbid you to build from its feed and exactly what licensed aggregators sell. And the platform rule agreed on 23-Aug for equities — no cross-user tick fan-out, no persisting user-session prices into platform datasets — applies unchanged: an exchange feed pulled under one consent cannot be re-broadcast to every user.

## 2. What SnapTrade gives for crypto, and what it does not

Through SnapTrade, Coinbase, Kraken and Binance are all "Read and Trade" and available immediately (plan §2.3): balances, positions with a last price, orders (90/90/30 days), activities since account open, and later trading (Coinbase with order preview; Kraken and Binance without). That is the full **account** picture. It is not market data: SnapTrade's developer terms bind us to use market data "only … to serve the End User for which that data is licensed by their broker" and never to "store, resell, or redistribute" it, and its own quote endpoint calls itself "not a substitute for a market data provider". So SnapTrade answers "what does this user hold and what did their exchange last price it at", for that user only — the same role it plays for equities.

## 3. Direct integration, venue by venue

### Coinbase — the India-relevant one

Coinbase is registered with FIU-IND (blog, 11-Mar-2025) and launched in India on 31-May-2026 with direct INR rails: "Indian customers can now deposit and withdraw INR via IMPS", spot plus crypto perpetuals, and "Coinbase Advanced offers professional-grade tools, including APIs built to institutional standards, WebSocket order book streaming". USDC-INR and BTC-INR pairs were added in Apr/May 2026. Accounts: OAuth2 is live (`login.coinbase.com/oauth2`, scopes `wallet:accounts:read`, `wallet:transactions:read`, and it reaches Advanced Trade `/brokerage/accounts`), but "OAuth client creation is currently limited to approved partners" — a request-access form; the ToS says eligibility requirements apply and Coinbase may terminate at its discretion. Market data: public Advanced Trade REST (`/market/products`, `/candles` max 350 buckets, `/ticker`, book) and most WebSocket channels need no key ("Most channels are now available without authentication"; unauthenticated WS messages "8 per second per IP") — but display to our users is barred by the Market Data Terms above. Fiat/stocks: US equities entered the Advanced Trade API on 29-Jul-2026 (`product_type=EQUITY`, "S&P 500 only", "candles … aren't available for equities yet"), US retail stock trading since Feb-2026 (Coinbase Capital Markets, Apex), UK since Aug-2026; tokenized stocks on Base for "eligible jurisdictions outside of the U.S." (custody by Alpaca under ADGM); stock perpetuals for non-US via a Deribit-powered gateway; prediction markets US-only. Whether Indian customers can reach any equity product: not found.

### Kraken — the most open door, the weakest India footing

Public market data needs no key (REST `/0/public/*` is `security: []`; "Authentication is not required to connect to the public market data feeds"); REST OHLC "Returns up to 720 of the most recent entries (older data cannot be retrieved)", with free quarterly OHLCVT dumps "from the beginning of each market". Accounts: Kraken Connect is a real OAuth 2.0 ("No more copying API keys and secrets!") with read scopes `account.fast-api-key:funds-query`, `trades-query-open/closed`, `ledger-query`; access/refresh tokens 24 h/30 d; registration needs an "Owner IBAN (must be linked to a verified business account)" and B2B approval. The API Partner Program (Jul-2026) is explicitly for terminals, bots and "Portfolio monitoring platforms", pays "Up to 50% lifetime commission", and says "xStocks are available through Kraken's spot and futures APIs". Fiat/stocks: forex spot pairs (EUR/USD, GBP/USD, USD/JPY …, non-US) and xStocks tokenized equities (`aclass_base=tokenized_asset`, pairs like `AAPLx/USD`; "Available to eligible non-U.S. clients in 110+ countries"; not US/CA/UK/AU; India neither excluded nor confirmed — `AssetPairs?country_code=IN` will answer it) are on the normal spot API; real US stocks/ETFs (Kraken Securities, US ex-ME/NY and the EEA) have no API. India: Kraken serves "clients residing in India" (a May-2026 India delisting notice proves it) and India is not on its prohibited list, but its licensing page (11-Sep-2026) shows no Indian registration, INR is not a supported currency, and a 2025 statement said it "intends to seek authorization to operate in India".

### Binance — biggest venue, most closed API posture

Market data: `data-api.binance.vision` / `data-stream.binance.vision` need no key ("The API key is not necessary"); limits per IP 6,000 request-weight/min (`exchangeInfo`), 300 WebSocket connections per 5 min, 1,024 streams per connection, 24 h connection life; klines "Default 500; max 1000"; free bulk history at data.binance.vision. Accounts: Binance Login (OAuth2) "is only provided to close ecosystem partners", and its documented scopes are identity-level (`user:openId`, `create:apikey`) — no balance scope found; the practical path is a user-created key ("Unrestricted-IP-Access HMAC API Key(s) won't have any permission other than reading"), which is what SnapTrade's Binance connection already collects. Fiat/stocks: fiat quote pairs (EUR, TRY, BRL, JPY; no INR pair); bStocks tokenized securities (issued by BTech Holdings, "offered through an Approved Prospectus in the ADGM and are not offered in any other jurisdiction"; `TSLABUSDT` is a normal SPOT symbol, so its public data flows with no key); US stocks/ETFs (Jul-2026) and US stock options (Sep-2026) via Nest Trading Limited (ADGM) with Alpaca clearing, API under `/sapi/v1/equity/*`, "permitted jurisdictions" unnamed. India: FIU-IND registered since Aug-2024 after an ₹18.82 crore penalty (FIU order 19-Jun-2024), PAN re-KYC in 2025, INR through P2P only; not among the 15 platforms FIU ordered blocked on 9-Sep-2026.

### Backpack — interesting shape, not a tier-1 rail yet

What it is: exchange (spot, perps, lending), self-custody wallet, and since Jun-2026 "Backpack Securities" — real US stocks and ETFs through a New-Zealand introducing broker (Backpack Securities Global Limited) → Atomic Vault Securities (SEC-registered) → RQD Clearing, held as "security entitlements under New York UCC Article 8", plus Solana-tokenized versions (20 tokens as of 10-Sep-2026) and stock perpetuals; prediction markets are an invite-only beta; "cards" appears on the homepage but no card product page was found. Licences: VARA (Dubai, spot only), CySEC MiFID II (the ex-FTX EU licence, reissued Jun-2025), MiCA CASP + PSD2 payment institution from Latvijas Banka (27-May-2026), FinCEN MSB, AUSTRAC. Scale: "$400B+ Volume" and "150+ Countries" are self-reported; CoinGecko showed ~$9.6M 24 h spot volume on 13-Sep-2026 (derivatives not shown); a $1B-valuation round was reported "in talks" (Feb-2026, Axios via The Block), closing not found. API: public markets/trades/klines/depth need no key; accounts via ED25519 keys with a "Read Only" toggle per sub-account (max 10); no OAuth; rate limits 2,000 req/min per sub-account, 30/min for time-range history endpoints; stocks tradable via RFQ in-session and an order book off-hours, `GET /api/v1/securities`, `stockPrice.<ticker>` stream. Regions: Backpack Securities "isn't available in the United States, the United Kingdom, the United Arab Emirates, or Japan, and isn't offered on Backpack EU"; India is on neither the not-served nor the coming-soon list, fiat is USD wire only, and there is no India statement at all.

## 4. Answer

For **live crypto prices shown on the platform** (holdings valuation for everyone, alerts, research, charts): do not build on an exchange's public feed, and do not build on SnapTrade. Technically trivial, contractually not ours to display — Coinbase forbids it in terms, Kraken and Backpack require written consent, Binance's API terms are unpublished and its policy bars commercial use without written authorisation. The correct source is a **licensed aggregator**, which is also the right product answer because it sells the composite reference price. Two things must be pinned before P30 runs: Tiingo's public pricing page marks "Tiingo Crypto" and the "Data Firehose (Crypto & IEX)" as "Internal Use Only" on both standard plans ("you may not display or share the data with another person"), so the $250/month Display-Redistribution order form from the 23-Aug decision must name crypto explicitly or crypto is not covered; and a second quote should be taken from a Coinbase-authorised redistributor (Polygon or CCData/CryptoCompare) or CoinGecko's commercial tier, because those licences are written for display. No prices for those are cited here — they were not researched today.

For **live prices for one authenticated user**, the equities rule carries over: the user's own venue may price the user's own holdings, shown to that user only, never stored into platform datasets. SnapTrade's position price does this for all three exchanges; a direct Coinbase OAuth or Kraken Connect token would do the same and adds nothing for pricing that justifies the partner-approval effort on its own.

For **accounts and trading**, SnapTrade remains the first rail for Coinbase, Kraken and Binance (P30–P33): one integration, read-and-trade, activities since account open, no per-exchange partner approvals. Two direct applications are worth filing in parallel because they buy things SnapTrade cannot: Kraken's API Partner Program (portfolio-monitoring category, commissions, xStocks and futures on the same API) and Coinbase's OAuth partner access (the only FIU-registered, INR-railed venue of the four, so the one Indian users will actually hold). Binance OAuth is closed to non-partners and gives identity only — skip. Backpack: watch, do not build — no OAuth, unproven volume, and no India statement.

On the **"crypto + fiat in one venue" idea for Indian users**: today no venue offers Indian residents stocks or fiat instruments with confirmed eligibility. Coinbase equities are US/UK; Kraken's real stocks are US/EEA and xStocks' India status is unconfirmed; Binance's US stocks and bStocks name no permitted jurisdictions; Backpack Securities is silent on India. Two eligibility probes are cheap and definitive — Kraken `GET /0/public/AssetPairs?aclass_base=tokenized_asset&country_code=IN` and Binance's bStocks "country eligibility endpoint" (referenced by Binance Academy, not yet located in the docs). Even where a venue turns out to be eligible, an Indian resident buying US securities through an offshore, non-SEBI venue funded via crypto raises FEMA/LRS and SEBI unregistered-platform questions that need counsel before anything is promoted; **monitoring** what a user already holds — the scope of this whole rail — is the safe side of that line and matches the 23-Aug stance.

## 5. Changes to the plan and prompts

P30 step 0 now reads: probe Tiingo crypto **only after** the order form confirms display rights for crypto; otherwise take a licensed aggregator quote and pin the source before building; never read exchange public feeds into platform storage. The plan's §3 table row for crypto is amended accordingly. New open items: Tiingo crypto rider; aggregator quote; Kraken API Partner application; Coinbase OAuth request-access; Kraken/Binance India eligibility probes; counsel note on offshore stock products for Indian residents.
