## 4. News reality check (Task 2 baseline)

**Stockbook today:** `news-rss-sync` (Google News RSS, 5-min market hours) + `news-sync` → `NewsArticle` (has `symbols[]/sectors/categories/sentiment` from its own tagging); per-stock `StockNews` (symbol+link unique) rendered in the stock News tab; `/discover/news` has a category `?filter=` param + client-side "My feeds" (`NewsFeed.filters` with symbols/sectors/keywords). **No first-class `?symbol=` deep-link param exists** — that's exactly what your "open the news section with the filter added" ask needs. `NewsItem` (M0) does not exist yet; nothing reads the claude-algo archive.

**claude-algo engine (migration source):** healthier and further along than the P12 prompt assumes. Deltas the prompt must absorb:

1. **Linker is v6**, not v3 (`LINKER_VERSION = "v6"`, per-version label keying, fails-closed `labelledBy` classifier). Ship bar unchanged and still unmet: **109 human pairs** (needs 300/lane, ≥0.95, ≥14 days) plus **320 machine labels** (`agent:claude-opus-5`, 13 FP) kept strictly apart — `precision()` reads only the human half.
2. **Two modules the P12 copy-list omits: `news_machine_judge.py` and `news_relink.py`** (the machine-labelling + re-linking harness), plus cli glue and their tests.
3. **Mongo migration list is too small:** P12 item 5 says "migrate the live FY collections"; it must also carry `news_links`, `news_link_labels` (the labels-back seed for P13 — losing it resets the ship bar to zero) and `news_daily` (digest history).
4. `by_symbol/` tier is **built but run-time-gated** on the ship bar — the gate semantics must survive migration verbatim; product must never read `by_symbol`, only the local store.
5. Filings lane is **not linked at all** (`symbols[]` empty means "nobody looked"); venue-vs-company (BSE/NSE/MCX), broker-as-issuer, foreign-parent classes remain string-unsolvable → they are the LLM-adjudication classes P13 exists for.
6. Cutover mechanics: claude-algo's writers are **systemd timers on the research VM** (`deploy/install_systemd.sh`, RUNBOOK) — the dual-write plan should name the actual units to disable, and the reverse grant (`algo-worker@` objectViewer) is already specified.

**Entity-news wiring (your spec, mapped to models):** per-entity sections — Latest 10 + Top 10, each capped, "view all" deep-links to `/discover/news` with the entity filter pre-applied — need: (a) a `NewsItem` store (M0), (b) a polymorphic link table (P13) shaped `{newsId, entityType: STOCK|AMC|MF_SCHEME|DEBT_ISSUER, entityKey, confidence, method}` rather than instrumentId-only, (c) a `?entity=` (or `?symbol=`) first-class param on `/discover/news`, and (d) a ranking rule for "Top" (pre-P13: source-tier × recency, deduped by canonical URL; post-P13: materiality × cluster size × confidence). **MF and Debt have no direct news source in the engine** — the honest semantics are: MF page = AMC-level news + a "Portfolio companies" rollup from its top holdings' adjudicated links; Debt = issuer-level via ISIN→issuer join. That is P13 linking work; P12's job is to not bake in a shape that precludes it.
