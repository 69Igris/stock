## Vision

### 10x Check

Today the product has deep data and shallow users: 668 registered, 1–3 real payers, 0 connected
brokers, no funnel data. The 10x version is the same data engine behind a loop that
works: connect any India or US broker and it stays connected, get an "aha" (roast or research)
in under 2 minutes, share it, pay in INR or USD, renew automatically, and every step is
measured. Most of the parts exist. The multiplier comes from fixing the joints between them,
not from building new engines.
