## AI Analysis (NEW guide)

**Content - written for users, no L1/L2/L3 internal details:**

### Quick Research (~30 seconds)

Just type the stock name:

- "Analyse RELIANCE"
- "Research TCS"
- "How is HDFC Bank doing?"

You'll get: Score, recommendation, key metrics, strengths & risks.

### Deep Analysis (~2 minutes)

Use keywords like "deep dive", "detailed", or "comprehensive":

- "Deep dive into RELIANCE"
- "Detailed analysis of TCS"

You'll get: Everything above + DCF valuation, cash flow quality, earnings
analysis, technical overview.

### Comprehensive Research (~5 minutes)

Use "comprehensive" for full research reports:

- "Comprehensive analysis of HDFC Bank"
- "Full research report on RELIANCE"

You'll get: Multi-page report with scenario analysis, peer comparison,
management quality, and investment thesis.

### Compare Stocks

- "Compare TCS vs INFY"
- "HDFC Bank vs ICICI Bank vs SBI"

### Tips

- Use @mention to tag stocks: "@RELIANCE what's the outlook?"
- Credits are consumed per query (shown in chat input)
- Deep analysis uses more credits but gives deeper insights
- Follow-up questions on the same analysis are free

## Stock Screening (NEW guide)

**Content:**

### Natural Language Screening

Type what you're looking for in plain English:

- "Find undervalued large caps with high ROE"
- "Stocks with RSI below 30 and PE under 15"
- "Growth stocks with revenue growth above 20%"

### Pre-Built Strategies

Choose from 28+ pre-built strategies:

- Value investing (PE, PB filters)
- Momentum (price breakouts, volume)
- Dividend yield (3%+ payers)
- Quality (high ROE, low debt)

### Combined Screens

Mix fundamental and technical criteria:

- "Undervalued stocks that are technically oversold"
- "High ROE companies breaking 52-week highs"
