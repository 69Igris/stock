## The Problem

India has **150M+ demat accounts** across 50+ brokers, yet no single platform
lets investors see their complete financial picture. Retail investors juggle
multiple broker apps, manually track trades in spreadsheets, and lack the
analytics tools that institutional investors take for granted. The result: poor
decision-making, missed tax-saving opportunities, and zero visibility into true
portfolio performance.

## The Solution

**UseMoney** is a unified portfolio intelligence platform that connects to all
major Indian brokers (Zerodha, Upstox, Groww, Angel One, Dhan, Fyers) and
provides:

- **One Dashboard** -- Aggregate holdings, positions, trades, and mutual funds
  across all brokers in real time
- **AI-Powered Insights** -- Gemini-powered chat that analyzes your portfolio
  and provides personalized recommendations
- **Smart Trade Analytics** -- Automated FIFO trade pairing, P&L tracking, and
  performance attribution
- **Paper Trading** -- Practice strategies with real market data, zero risk
- **FIRE Planning** -- Retirement and financial independence calculators
  tailored to Indian investors
