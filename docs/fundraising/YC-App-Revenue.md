### What we understand that they don't

1. **The multi-broker problem is real and unsolved.** Every competitor is locked
   to one broker ecosystem. Multibagg does research but can't see your portfolio
   across Zerodha + Angel One + Groww. Sensibull is Zerodha-only. We're the only
   cross-broker intelligence layer.

2. **AI analysis without execution is half the product.** Multibagg lets you
   research; we let you research AND act. Finny gives you a FIRE plan; we give
   you the plan AND connect to your brokers to execute it. The gap between
   "insight" and "action" is where most retail investors lose money.

3. **The agent/professional angle is a $0 market today.** India has ~135K
   financial professionals (RIAs + RAs + MF distributors) managing client money,
   and NONE of them have AI-powered multi-client portfolio management tools.
   Multibagg and Finny are consumer-only. We can sell agent seats at $60/mo -
   each agent manages 50-500 clients, so one agent seat = massive downstream
   value.

4. **SEBI's retail algo framework creates a greenfield category.** No competitor
   has shipped SEBI-compliant retail algo execution with AI strategy generation.
   We will be first.
