## The problem

India has **216M demat accounts** and **136M unique investors**, served by **fewer than 1,000 SEBI-registered advisors**. Retail investors juggle 2–3 broker apps, track trades in spreadsheets, and get dashboards while institutions get analysts. Real portfolio intelligence (performance attribution, risk, tax) simply doesn't exist for retail, and advice can't scale with humans.

## The product

UseMoney connects **all of a user's brokers**, with 6 live integrations (Zerodha _audited & approved_, Upstox, Groww, Angel One, Dhan, Fyers), reconciles every trade (FIFO pairing, tax lots), and puts a **hedge-fund-grade AI analyst on the user's real portfolio**:

- **Research that cites its sources:** stocks + mutual funds, deterministic math underneath, not LLM guesses; multi-stock/fund comparison built in
- **Portfolio intelligence:** "Why did I underperform NIFTY this quarter?" → attribution, factor exposure, stress scenarios on _your_ verified holdings
- **Memory layer** (shipping now): learns your trade history, P&L and mistakes over 3–6 months; advice becomes personal
- **Strategy Builder** (in dev): idea → backtest in **under 24 hours**, vs the 3–6 months it takes retail today

**No financial knowledge required.** Chat replaces screeners, jargon and paid data subscriptions. What no-code did for building software, UseMoney does for investing.

## Why we win

1. **The money graph.** Verified holdings + paired trade history across 6 brokers. Brokers structurally cannot build this for each other's accounts; generic AI chat can't see it at all. Switching cost compounds with every synced trade.

3. **Velocity.** Entire platform built solo: 6 broker integrations, a 118-tool research agent, 22K passing tests, ~30 PRs in the last 20 days.
